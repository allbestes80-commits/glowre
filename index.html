Before your next shower

Why does an oil turn to milk?

The oils are blended with gentle cleansers that wait for water. When you massage the oil on wet skin, the two mix into a light milky lather, which rinses away with the dirt.

What does it smell like?

Warm sweet almond with a little bergamot. It stays softly on the skin after you rinse.

Do I still need body lotion?

Many people skip it on busy nights, because skin does not feel tight after rinsing. On very dry days you can still add your usual lotion.

Is it all right for sensitive skin?

It is gentle, but it contains fragrance and an oil from nuts. If you react to either, try a little on the inside of your arm first.

What is inside?

Vitis Vinifera (Grape) Seed Oil, TIPA-Laureth Sulfate, Laureth-3, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Parfum, Cocamide MEA, Propylene Glycol, Sorbitan Oleate, Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil, Citrus Aurantium Bergamia (Bergamot) Fruit Oil, Helianthus Annuus (Sunflower) Seed Oil, Tocopherol, Aqua, Rosmarinus Officinalis Leaf Extract, Limonene, Coumarin, Linalool.

Anything to watch out for?

For external use only; keep it away from your eyes. Oil can make the shower floor slippery, so rinse it after use. In a cold room the oil may look a little cloudy; it clears at room temperature.
